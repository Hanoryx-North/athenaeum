# Unified Athenaeum workspace

Mode: Operate; wiki content is Read. Audience: local project users, Library readers and service operators. Scope: shared primitives, application shells, authentication, profile/account entry points and common component styling across all three applications.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One recognizable workspace from local wiki to shared Library to service administration. Preserve the current identity while replacing competing shells and cramped account flows.

OWN-WORLD: Roboto with a cool slate canvas, blue navigation/selection, green productive actions, 12px panels, 7px controls, quiet separators and labelled navigation.

STORY: Find your project or wiki, understand its state, complete the task. Account identity is always reachable; sign-in has a dedicated screen and a clear return path.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 64?68px brand/identity header, a labelled rail on wide screens and a flexible work area. Page titles sit above the controls they scope. Auth uses a two-column brand/context and form composition, stacked on phones.

FORM: Code-led application design; user-pinned incumbent baseline. Signature move: one account identity and full-page authentication layout across local, public and operator surfaces. Motion is limited to responsive control feedback with reduced-motion support. seed: none (user-pinned baseline).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
